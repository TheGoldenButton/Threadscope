Desktop: drag to orbit, wheel to zoom, WASD/QE to fly, click concepts to select several (selected words pulse; click again to deselect). With words selected, Play puts you inside the current, flying node to node in resonant order. Every word you type is searched, including common words like the or not; only the drawn field hides them. VR: a query bar, keyboard and a results box float in view (point and pinch or trigger the bar to type). Mixed reality (Quest 3): press Enter mixed reality to see your room through the passthrough cameras. Pinch and drag anywhere to move the data sphere, pinch with both hands and spread to resize, quick pinch on a concept to select it; select several to see how they connect, and pinch a selected word again to deselect it. The field remembers where you put it between mixed-reality sessions. Sticks fly in VR. A adds the selected concept to the query, B clears. Press Place on the panel to snap the field onto a wall, table or floor. Brighter nodes are more frequent words. The field learns Hebbian-style: words you query or select together are pulled closer and their links glow, and the arrangement is saved per text. Line colour runs violet to cyan to amber to white as a connection gets stronger. Once you have a query or selection, a Play button, timeline and speed button appear: words appear one by one, strongest resonance first (the speed button cycles 0.25x to 4x). The results box scrolls: thumbstick while pointing at it, or pinch-drag it; tap a row to select that word.
